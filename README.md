Use Cases:
-----------
When the operator hits button 1 on the numberpad, the elevator will jog down.

When the operator hits button 2 on the numberpad, the elevator will jog up.

When the operator hits button 3 on the numberpad, the elevator will move to the height of the coral station, the coral arm will move to the intake position, and the limelight will align with the branch of the reef.

When the operator hits button 4 on the numberpad, the elevator will move to position 0, the coral arm will move to the outtake position, and the limelight will align with the branch of the reef.

When the operator hits button 5 on the numberpad, the elevator will move to level 1, the coral arm will move to the outtake position, and the limelight will align with the branch of the reef.

When the operator hits button 6 on the numberpad, the elevator will move to level 2, the coral arm will move to the outtake position, and the limelight will align with the branch of the reef.

When the operator hits button 7 on the numberpad, the elevator will move to level 3, the coral arm will move to the outtake position, and the limelight will align with the branch of the reef.

When the operator hits button 8 on the numberpad, the elevator will move to level 4, the coral arm will move to the outtake position, and the limelight will align with the branch of the reef.
